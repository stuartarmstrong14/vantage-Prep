Day one

Getting started

  1. Go to www.145prepped.co.uk on your phone, tablet or computer.
  2. Sign in with the username and starting password your manager gave you.
  3. Change your password straight away: tap Account, fill in Change password, then Save new password.
  4. Add it to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app. On iPhone (Safari) tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android (Chrome) tap the three-dot menu, then Install app.

Kinds of login

  • Staff add cars, tick off prep, upload 145 point checks, write notes, print QR stickers and archive completed cars.
  • Technician logins see every car but can only tick their own job: Valet Technician (Valet and Photos), Paint Technician, Alloy Technician (who can also set how many wheels need refining) and Dent Technician.
  • Advisors can look but not change anything, for example after scanning a car's QR sticker.
  • Administrators can do everything staff can, plus Reports, deleting cars and managing staff logins.

Home screen

Finding a car

The stock is split into three tabs. Tap any car card to open it.

  • In prep: cars still being worked on.
  • Completed: cars with every box ticked, including the final quality check.
  • Archived: cars moved out of the way.

To find any car, type a registration, tag number, make or model into Search our stock and tap Search. It looks through every car, including Completed and Archived, and labels each result. Tap Clear search to go back.

Each card shows the registration (YD74 XXM), days in prep, the prep requested (TAC Service MOT), the six-stage bar and how many of the 15 tasks are done.

How prep is going shows cars in prep, cars completed, and the average days in prep against the 7-day target.

Home screen

Department boxes

Under How prep is going, Still to do, by department shows a box for each department with the number of cars that still need it.

  • A car counts in every department it still needs, whatever stage it has reached. A car at Valet with its mechanical boxes unticked counts under Mechanical too.
  • Tap Bodywork and the Bodywork jobs buttons open underneath, counting cars still needing Cosmetic, Dents, Alloy wheels and Paint. Tap one to go straight to that job; tap it again to go back to all Bodywork.
  • Tap a box to show only those cars. The box turns navy and the stock list says what you are viewing.
  • Tap the box again, or tap Show all, to see every car.
The car sheet

Working on a car

Open the car and tick each job as it is done. Everything saves as you type or tick, so there is no Save button.

  • Stage bar: green ticks are finished stages; the dark segment is where the car is now.
  • Sign-offs: ticking Bodywork QC complete or the final Quality check completed adds your initials and the date.
  • Who and when: every ticked box shows the date and the person who ticked it.
  • Car details: correct the registration, make, model, tag, mileage or MOT expiry at the top. Type the MOT date as digits, for example 250527, and it becomes 25/05/27.
  • Notes: faults, parts on order, who has the keys.

When all prep is done, the car waits at Quality Check (it shows in the Quality Check department box). Once the final Quality check completed box is ticked, it moves to the Completed tab by itself.

Mechanical and Bodywork

145 point checks and cosmetic notes

145 point mechanical check

This is done by uploading the paperwork, not by ticking a box.

  1. Open the car and go to Mechanical.
  2. Tap Upload check and choose a PDF or take a photo (JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, up to 15 MB).
  3. The row changes to Completed ✓ with the file name.

Tap Add another file for extra pages, up to 10 per car. To remove a file tap Remove, then Tap again to remove. Removing the last file sets the check back to Needs upload.

Cosmetic notes and wheels

Under Cosmetic check complete on the Bodywork stage, write what cosmetic work the car needs, for example "respray rear bumper, door ding nearside". Tick the box when the check is done.

Next to Alloy wheels completed, enter how many alloys need refining (0 to 5).

The notes and wheel counts feed the Outstanding bodywork lists in Reports, so painters and managers can see every job by tag number.

New stock

Adding cars

By hand

Tap Add car, fill in registration, make and model (tag number and mileage if you have them), tap the Prep requested buttons that apply, then Add to prep. The car starts at Inbound.

By email

Send the car's details to the 145 Prepped email-in address (your manager has it). The car appears at Inbound within a minute with the note "Added from email. Check the details are right." Put each detail on its own line:

To: 145 Prepped email-in addressSubject: anything
TOYOTA
AYGO X
YD74XXM
TAG T1042
TAC
SERVICE
MOT - 25/05/2027
MILEAGE 13442
  • Put the number on the TAG line, for example TAG T1042. A TAG line with no number brings in no tag.
  • TAC, SERVICE and MOT lines fill Prep requested. An MOT line with a date also sets the MOT expiry.
  • A registration already on the system, including completed and archived cars, is skipped rather than duplicated.

Always open a car added by email and check its details.

On the car

QR stickers and the Awaiting prep notice

Every car has its own QR code. Scanning it with a phone camera opens that car's progress after the person signs in, so advisors can check a car on the forecourt.

  1. Open the car and tap Print QR sticker.
  2. Choose a layout and tap Print.
  3. Fit it, then tick QR sticker fitted on the Inbound stage.

In the print window, turn headers and footers off and keep the scale at 100%. The notice says the QR is for staff, so customers are not sent to a sign-in page.

After prep

Completed, archived and live updates

  • Completed: a car moves here by itself once every box is ticked, including the final quality check. Until then it waits in the Quality Check box. Unticking a box sends it back to In prep.
  • Archiving: open a completed car and tap Archive car. Cars are also archived automatically 30 days after they are completed, or 365 days after they arrived. Nothing is deleted, archived cars still count in Reports, and Restore from archive brings one back.
  • Live updates: when someone else ticks a box, uploads a check or adds a car, a pop-up appears at the top of your screen within about 8 seconds. Tap it to close it. You never see pop-ups for your own changes, and only while the app is open.
Managers

For administrators

Tap Reports in the header. It updates live and includes archived cars.

  • Top figures: cars completed, average days from arrival to completed, the share done within 7 days, and cars over target.
  • Cars completed by week, month or year, or Pick a month.
  • Where the stock is now, time in each stage, cars over target, cars sent back and sign-offs by person.
  • Outstanding bodywork (near the bottom): Alloys lists cars with wheels to refine and the total; Cosmetics lists cars with notes and paint not done. Tag number first; tap a car to open it.
  • 145 point checks (at the bottom): type a registration to find and view its check.

Staff logins

Tap Account, then Manage staff logins. Add a person with their full name, a username, a starting password and a role, then tell them the password in person. From the list you can change a role, reset a password, deactivate a login or delete it. Past sign-offs stay on the cars.

Data health

Tap Account, then Data health and recovery. It shows that every 145 point check is stored and attached to its car. If a check is stored but not showing, tap Reattach to their cars. Removed checks stay in the Recycle bin for 30 days and can be restored. To bring back checks from an older copy, choose a downloaded backup under Recover checks from a backup.

Backups

Tap Account, then Download backup. It saves one file with everything: cars, history, logins and uploaded 145 point checks. Do it once a week and keep the file somewhere private, such as a work drive, because it includes staff logins. The app also saves its own copy every day and before every update.

Deleting a car

Open it and tap Delete car twice. This also deletes its 145 point check files and cannot be undone, so archive instead unless the car was added by mistake.
